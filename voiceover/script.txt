Nobody taught you how money works in school. But it affects every decision you'll ever make. Let's fix that in sixty seconds.
Financial literacy is simply knowing how to earn, spend, save, and grow your money. Start with one rule: spend less than you earn. It sounds obvious, but most people never track where their money goes.
Try the fifty, thirty, twenty rule. Fifty percent of your income goes to needs, like rent and food. Thirty percent goes to wants. Twenty percent goes to savings and debt payoff.
Build an emergency fund that covers three to six months of expenses. And pay off high-interest debt first. Credit card interest can cost you more than your investments earn.
Then let time work for you. Compound interest means your money earns money, and that money earns more. Starting small today beats starting big ten years from now.
